Privacy Policy

Whose Kitten? · 한국어 · Account & data deletion

1. What this game collects

If you sign in, we store it with your account. If you don't, it stays on your device.

ItemWhatWhy
Account identifier The unique identifier issued by the sign-in provider when you sign in with a Google account (Google Play Games) So we can find your save again on a different device
Game progress Your bells and gems, the cats and case records in your shelter, the day you are on So you can continue after reinstalling or switching devices
Purchase history What you bought and when, the gems granted, and the purchase token issued by the store (used to verify the receipt) To confirm a purchase really happened, to make sure you don't lose what you paid for, and to check against your question if you write to us
Device information App version, OS version, device type, device language To find bugs and see which devices they happen on

We never ask for your name, phone number, address or date of birth. An email address is used only to reply to a question you send us. The game does not access your photo library, contacts, location or microphone. Photos made by the album feature are captures of the game screen, kept in the app's own folder on your device — they are never uploaded.

If you play without signing in, all of the above is stored on your device only (save.json). In that case deleting the app deletes your progress.

2. Advertising

The game shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob. They play only when you press the button — there are no pop-up interstitial ads.

To serve ads, Google may process device information (advertising identifier, IP address, device type, approximate region). See policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

This game is rated for everyone and is configured on the assumption that children may play it. The Google Mobile Ads SDK is therefore requested with:

  • child-directed treatment (tag_for_child_directed_treatment)
  • under the age of consent (tag_for_under_age_of_consent)
  • maximum ad content rating G

Under these settings no personalised ads are served and no advertising identifier is used for tracking. That is also why no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown on iOS — there is no tracking to ask about.

In the EEA and the UK, consent is handled through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). With the "under the age of consent" flag set, no consent form is shown and only non-personalised ads are served.

3. Usage analytics

To see where players get stuck and which features are used, the game records in-game events (screen entered, case solved or failed, ad watched, and similar) through Google Analytics for Firebase. App version, device type, device language and an app instance identifier are recorded with them. No information that identifies you personally is included.

4. Payments

Payment itself is handled by the store (Google Play · Apple App Store). This game never sees or stores payment instrument details, including card numbers.

We do receive the purchase token the store issues. We use it to ask the store whether the purchase is genuine before granting gems, and we record what was bought and when against your account. That record is what lets your purchases follow you to a new device, and what prevents double grants and fraudulent purchases.

6. What we don't do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not identify or track players for advertising purposes.
  • We do not combine your activity from other apps or websites.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Account and game progress: kept while the account exists, and deleted on request — see account & data deletion.
  • Purchase records: under Korean e-commerce law, records of payment and supply of goods are kept for 5 years and records of consumer complaints or disputes for 3 years. During those periods those records remain even after a deletion request.
  • On-device storage: deleted when you delete the app.
  • You can reset or switch off your device's advertising identifier in the OS settings — Android: Settings → Google → Ads / iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

8. Children

This game is rated for everyone and children may play it. We do not separately collect personal information such as a name or contact details from children, and we ask children for nothing beyond what section 1 describes. Deletion for a child's data can be requested through the same channel as section 7.
